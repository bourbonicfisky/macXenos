# macXenos 1.0 beta 5 — release notes

macXenos is an unofficial macOS port of Xenia Canary with a native Metal
rendering backend. This build is tagged `macxenos-1.0-beta5` on the
`codex/metal-foundation-integration` trunk. It follows beta 4 (2026-10-10);
see `1.0-beta4.md` for everything beta 4 added.

## What changed since beta 4

### Faster games, less stutter

- **Much less stutter after an update.** macXenos now records the shaders
  and pipelines each game uses and rebuilds them while the game loads, and
  keeps them in an archive that survives other games filling the macOS
  shader cache. Mafia II on an M1 Max, first session after an update:
  in-play build pauses 4.1 s -> 0.16 s, for about 2.2 s more loading (the
  rebuild uses several cores; Rise of the Tomb Raider, the largest in the
  smoke test, 38.6 s on one core -> 7.3 s). Later
  sessions: Red Dead Redemption 1,250 -> 282 ms of pauses, Halo: Reach
  892 -> 47 ms, GTA IV 461 -> 22 ms. `XE_METAL_SHADER_STORAGE=0` and
  `metal_pipeline_binary_archive = false` turn the two parts off.
- **Game threads run on the performance cores by default.** macOS had been
  parking most of them on efficiency cores. On an M4 Pro with default
  settings, Mafia II went from 35.6 to 150.6 fps and GTA IV from 39.8 to
  60. Existing configs switch over too; **Graphics > Performance cores >
  Off** restores the old behaviour.
- **Resident Evil 5 runs at 29 fps instead of 12** on an M4 mini: games
  waiting on several events at once now wake immediately instead of
  polling every millisecond. `threading_blocking_waitall = false` (Advanced
  > Threading) restores the poll.
- **Frame-rate gains in specific titles**, most of them now at the game's
  own cap ("Before" is the first measurement this cycle; several of these
  games did not get this far in beta 4):

  | Game | Mac | Before | Now |
  | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
  | Burnout Paradise | M1 Max / M4 mini | ~34 / ~47 | 60 / 60 |
  | Rage | M1 Max / M4 mini | 14.8 / 30 | 60 / 60 |
  | Fable II | M1 Max | 20.6 | 30 |
  | Banjo-Kazooie: Nuts & Bolts | M1 Max | 20 | 30 |
  | Far Cry 3 | M4 mini | 20 | 30 |
  | Rise of the Tomb Raider | M4 mini | 23 | 30 |
  | Grand Theft Auto IV (no cap) | M4 mini | ~35 | 54 |
  | Mafia II (no cap) | M4 mini | 81 | 108 |

- **Uncapped games can be held to 60.** GTA IV and Mafia II set no frame
  rate of their own and run as fast as the Mac allows. **Graphics > Uncapped
  games > 60 FPS** holds them to 60 to match your display and save power.
- **Intel Macs with AMD graphics** (Mac Pro, RX 6900 XT):
  - Faster: Skate 2 10 -> 20 fps, Viva Piñata 6 -> 30, Ultra Street
    Fighter IV, LEGO Star Wars II and Call of Duty 2 30 -> 60, Just Cause 2
    20 -> 29.6, Gears of War 3 20 -> 28, Red Dead Redemption 14 -> 21.
  - Fixed: Red Dead's streaked title-screen foliage, lines along the edges
    of Gears of War 3 characters, the Viva Piñata and Skate 2 crashes, and
    Burnout Paradise crashing at boot.

### Games that now start or keep running

- **Forza Horizon 1 and 2** froze on the brightness screen every time; both
  now reach the opening drive at 30 fps.
- **Halo 4** aborted at boot. It now reaches its menus, its prologue video
  plays in colour instead of freezing, and the opening cryo bay renders.
- **Rise of the Tomb Raider** no longer sits on its autosave notice forever.
- **Crashes fixed:** Fable II when its world loads, Burnout Paradise after
  its first load, Mass Effect after about seven minutes (and while naming
  your character), Gears of War: Judgment 7-17 minutes into Survival, and
  Ultra Street Fighter IV at boot (about one launch in five).
- **Banjo-Kazooie: Nuts & Bolts** no longer freezes on a white screen at
  boot or hangs at its first level (each hit one run in three or more).
- **Any game could stall at random while loading** (seen as Gears of War 3
  stuck on "Loading profile"): a new game thread could fail to start.

### Graphics fixes

- **Crysis 3:** the first mission no longer washes out to white or explodes
  into streaks.
- **Rise of the Tomb Raider:** no more black world or green/teal casts, and
  the dark sheet that stretched from Lara's hood in half the frames is gone.
- **Grand Theft Auto IV:** headlights and street lamps are back, at full
  brightness.
- **Halo: Reach:** briefing faces render (they were floating eyes, teeth and
  hair). **Halo 3:** no white flashes in the pod-drop intro.
- **Forza Horizon 1 and 2:** proper skies instead of coloured bands.
- **Fable II:** no white smear or blacked-out patch around the hero; the
  breadcrumb trail shows. **Gears of War: Judgment:** no longer bleached
  and striped.
- **One-frame glitches gone:** Mass Effect's colour wraps and vanishing
  characters, Resident Evil 5's flickering tree shadows, and Rage's walls
  dropping to black (Rage's texture seams are fixed too).
- **Viva Piñata:** grass no longer draws as streaks.
- **Fewer missing objects:** draws were silently skipped when a buffer
  filled (267,000 in one GTA IV run); macXenos now waits for room.

### Stability

- A damaged or partly copied disc image shows an error instead of crashing.
- A crashed or force-quit session no longer keeps its memory tied up until
  you restart the Mac.
- One game's built-in settings no longer leak into your config and slow
  down every game after it.
- Games run silently instead of crashing when no audio device opens.
- Vulkan no longer crashes on Macs migrated from Intel with an old
  Intel-only MoltenVK installed.
- Crash and hang logs now describe what each game thread was doing.
- Fixes from Xenia Canary and Edge, among them profiles with a language set
  reading as parental-controlled.

### App and settings

- **Software updates.** macXenos can check macxenos.com for a new version
  and offer to install it (**macXenos > Check for Updates…**; schedule under
  **System > Software update**). It asks first, and installs nothing
  without asking.
- **Your files moved to standard macOS folders.** Saves are now in
  `~/Documents/macXenos/Saves`. Everything else (settings, profiles, title
  updates, installs, the shader cache, the log) is in
  `~/Library/Application Support/macXenos`. The first launch copies
  `~/.local/share/Xenia` across and leaves the original as a backup.
- **New settings:**
  - **Audio > Volume**: game volume, without changing your Mac's volume.
  - **System > Game saves** and **Game installs**: choose where these
    folders live.
  - **Graphics > Unset shader inputs** (Auto fixes Resident Evil 5's
    shadows); under **Troubleshooting**, **Memory exports from drawing
    shaders** (experimental) and **Finish each GPU submission first** (see
    Known issues).
- **System > About > Acknowledgements** lists the open-source software
  macXenos includes, with each license.

## What you get

- `macXenos.app`, a Universal 2 bundle (Apple Silicon + Intel), signed with
  a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, distributed as
  `macXenos-1.0-beta5.dmg`. Drag it to Applications.
- Requires macOS 15 or newer.
- Shaders are translated DXBC -> DXIL -> Metal at runtime through the
  bundled dxilconv and Apple Metal Shader Converter libraries. The first
  run of a title compiles pipelines and is slower than later runs.

## First run

- Settings, profiles, caches and the log live in
  `~/Library/Application Support/macXenos`; saves in
  `~/Documents/macXenos/Saves`. Put an empty `portable.txt` next to the
  executable inside the bundle to keep everything beside the app.
- Open a game from the dashboard's Home or All Games, with File > Open, or
  from a terminal: `open -n /Applications/macXenos.app --args "/path/to/game.iso"`.
- Controllers: any SDL-recognised gamepad works out of the box. A Guide tap
  opens the dashboard (see Known issues for macOS 26).
- Keyboard: off by default; set `keyboard_mode = 1` to make the keyboard
  act as controller 1 (bindings under `[HID.Key]`, listed in the beta 4
  notes).

## Known issues

- **Guide/Home button on macOS 26.** The system Games overlay takes the
  controller's Home button before the app sees it. Open the dashboard with
  F6 or Cmd-comma, then set **Controller > Dashboard shortcut** to
  **Back + Start**.
- **Rise of the Tomb Raider** shows a rare whole-screen colour flash, about
  once every ten minutes of play on an M1 Max; the cause is still open.
  **Graphics > Troubleshooting > Finish each GPU submission first** removes
  it. It costs frame rate on an M1 Max (30 -> 21 fps); M4 Macs usually stay
  at 30. Leave it off for other games.
- **Prince of Persia: The Forgotten Sands** sometimes stalls on its first
  loading screen. Quit and start it again.
- **Crysis 3** has frozen during gameplay in a few long test runs. If it
  happens, please send the log.
- **Intel Macs with AMD GPUs** are tested less and are slower than Apple
  silicon. Some texture formats Apple GPUs read directly are not available
  there. Set `gpu = vulkan` (MoltenVK) if a title misbehaves.
- **Higher internal resolutions.** 2x2 runs well below 1x on an M4 (Just
  Cause 2 18.4 fps); 3x3 is playable only on lighter titles.
- **The first session with a new game** still pauses while its shaders are
  built. Later sessions are much smoother, and so is the first session after
  a future update for any game played on beta 5 (games not yet recorded
  rebuild their shaders once more).
- **Multi-disc titles.** Disc swaps open a file picker; install discs (GTA
  V, Forza 4 content disc) do not run on their own.
- **Memory.** One instance at a time on a 16 GB machine; a session uses
  2 to 3.5 GB and grows with the shader cache.

## Reporting problems

Attach `~/Library/Application Support/macXenos/xenia.log` and say which
backend the title bar showed. For a freeze, set `log_fps_interval = 5` in
the config before playing and leave the game frozen for 30 seconds: the log
then records what every game thread is waiting on.
For rendering issues, a GPU trace captured with F4 (GPU > Trace Frame) is
the most useful artifact.
