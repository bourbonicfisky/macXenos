# macXenos 1.0 beta 7 — release notes

macXenos is an unofficial macOS port of Xenia Canary with a native Metal
rendering backend. This build is tagged `macxenos-1.0-beta7` on the
`codex/metal-foundation-integration` trunk. It follows beta 6 (2026-10-29);
see `1.0-beta6.md` for everything beta 6 added.

## What changed since beta 6

- **Ridge Racer Unbounded draws the whole screen.** The lower half of every
  race showed only fog and a dark outline of the car. The game draws its
  picture in two horizontal bands and keeps each band's depth separately;
  macXenos now joins the two, so the road, the car and the street render to
  the bottom edge. (The very first frame after the race starts can still
  show the old split.)
- **Halo 4: Cortana has her face back.** In the opening cryo-bay scene she
  appeared as hair and glowing eyes only. Halo 4 now gets the same treatment
  as Halo: Reach, whose missing faces had the same cause.
