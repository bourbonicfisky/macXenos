# macXenos 1.0.2 — release notes

This patch fixes loss of image detail when using higher internal resolutions
in **Grand Theft Auto IV**.

At 2x and 3x, a full-screen image used during rendering could fall back to
native resolution. The final picture was larger, but edges and fine details
remained coarse, even with FXAA enabled. macXenos now preserves the higher
resolution through this step, producing cleaner edges and clearer detail.

The fix was checked at 2x on a base M4 and 3x on an M5 Max, with FXAA enabled,
and with a native-resolution control. It does not change your graphics
settings or promise a frame-rate improvement.

All other changes and known issues are unchanged from
[1.0.1](1.0.1.md).
