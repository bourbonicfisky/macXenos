# macXenos 1.1 — release notes

macXenos is an unofficial macOS port of Xenia Canary with a native Metal
renderer. It runs Xbox 360 games on Apple Silicon Macs, and on Intel Macs
with an AMD GPU (Radeon RX 580 with 4 GB or better). It needs macOS 15 or
later. This release follows 1.0.2 (2026-11-17).

## Install / update

If you have 1.0 or later, choose **macXenos > Check for Updates…**, or wait
for the scheduled check (System > Software update in Preferences). Otherwise
download `macXenos-1.1.dmg`, open it and drag macXenos to Applications.
The app is a Universal app, signed and notarized by Apple. Your settings,
saves and game library carry over.

## What changed since 1.0.2

This release offers roughly an 8–15% speed increase in frame rates, making
2x (1440p) run at full speed in many games on a base Mac mini M4, increases
visual fidelity for 2x and above, and includes both broad compatibility
fixes and per-title fixes.

### Higher resolutions (2x and 3x)

- Faster, cleaner 2x and 3x rendering across the Metal backend: lossless
  render-target compression is now on by default, paired with fused
  resolves, tighter render passes, and preserved HDR and packed-color
  detail.
- **Gears of War**, **Gears of War 2** and **Mass Effect** at 2x and 3x:
  characters' faces and shadows are lit cleanly instead of breaking into
  dark triangles.
- **Burnout Revenge:** menu and event videos no longer flicker black above
  1x.
- **Dead Space 3:** colors render cleanly at 2x and 3x.
- Scaled depth-of-field blur stays sharp at higher internal resolutions
  instead of stretching a 1x pass.

### Graphics

- **Grand Theft Auto IV:** shadows no longer flicker on Apple Silicon.
- **Forza Motorsport 4:** the cockpit windshield now shows the track ahead.
- **Lara Croft and the Guardian of Light**, **Fable III**, **LEGO Marvel
  Super Heroes**, **LEGO Marvel's Avengers**, **LEGO Pirates of the
  Caribbean**, **Battlefield 3**, **The Darkness II** and **Call of Duty:
  Advanced Warfare:** missing, garbled or corrupted surfaces are fixed.
- **Darksiders** and **Darksiders II:** character and world geometry no
  longer breaks apart across frame boundaries.
- **Assassin's Creed III:** world and object pop-in is fixed.
- **Dark:** the club interior no longer renders black.
- **Kingdoms of Amalur: Reckoning:** the lower tile of the image renders
  cleanly.
- Window scissor offsets are now honored on Metal, fixing clipped screens
  and HUD elements in games that render with non-zero window offsets.
- **Intel Macs with AMD Radeon RX 580X:** stencil cache rebuilds only
  missing rows on a miss instead of rebuilding the full surface.

### Stability, audio and compatibility

- **NHL 15 (and other EA titles):** each game now gets its own isolated
  `cache:` partition by title ID (matching Xbox 360 utility-partition
  behavior), preventing titles that share cache filenames (`data0.big`,
  `bootcache.big`, etc.) from loading another game's cache files and
  crashing (such as **NHL 15** crashing after playing **SSX**).
- **F.E.A.R. 3:** no longer hangs on an infinite loading spinner when
  entering Single Player (overlapped operations now clear their internal
  context field on completion, matching Xbox 360 XAM behavior).
- **Test Drive Unlimited 2:** boots cleanly to the title screen and into
  gameplay (fixed POSIX socket polling bounds, error propagation, guest
  `setsockopt` width/endianness translation and `WSAEventSelect`).
- **Grand Theft Auto V:** no longer freezes in the prologue office when
  moving.
- **Call of Duty: Black Ops II:** local and split-screen play now works
  cleanly.
- **Assassin's Creed II:** no longer spins five CPU cores idle-looping on
  `Sleep(0)`.
- **Ridge Racer 6:** save files are no longer flagged as corrupt.
- **OutRun Online Arcade:** audio clicking is fixed (XMA contexts now trim
  samples requested by frame headers).
- **Forza Motorsport 4:** Disc 2 content installation works, and unnumbered
  `(Game Disc)` / `(Install Disc)` sets are grouped automatically as one
  multi-disc game.
- **Games on Demand (SVOD)** packages now launch cleanly from exFAT and
  network drives (ignoring macOS `._*` AppleDouble metadata files).
- **Guardians of Middle-Earth**, **Haunted House**, **Blitz: The League**,
  **Command & Conquer 3**, **Samurai Shodown II** and **Darksiders II:**
  boot crashes, hangs and post-load frame-pacing issues are resolved.
- **59 upstream Xenia Canary and Edge fixes** covering XAM and kernel
  return codes, byte order, file info queries, non-ASCII package filenames
  and missing exports, alongside further Xbox 360 hardware parity fixes for
  waits, timers and thread priorities.

### App and localization

- **17 languages:** the dashboard and macOS menus are now available in
  English, Danish, German, Spanish, Finnish, French, Italian, Japanese,
  Korean, Norwegian Bokmål, Dutch, Polish, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian,
  Swedish, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese.
- **Color-vision accessibility:** new Red–green and Blue–yellow interface
  palettes under **System > Dashboard > Color vision** (applies to both day
  and night dashboard themes; game colors are unchanged).
- Pressing **Escape**, **Guide** or **F6** on the Home Screen with no game
  open no longer blanks the window.
- **396 titles tested** across **399 automated gameplay routes** (338
  retail/GoD and 58 XBLA), with **305 games** currently published in the
  [compatibility list](../compatibility.md) (up from 228 in 1.0.1) and 80
  titles benchmarked at 1x, 2x and 3x across M4, M4 Pro, M1 Max and M5 Max
  Macs.

## Technical highlights

### Verified against physical Xbox 360 hardware

- **Hardware-reference parity:** Added paired kernel, networking and GPU
  probes run directly on a physical Xbox 360 console and verified macXenos
  against the recorded hardware outputs.
- **Waits, timers, priorities and APCs:** Matched console behavior for
  absolute-time waits and delays, immediate signaling of `KeSetTimer` /
  `KeSetTimerEx` and handle timers armed in the past, base thread priority
  offsets (`KeQueryBasePriorityThread` / `KeSetBasePriorityThread`),
  canceling or rearming pending timer APCs, rejecting `NtQueueApcThread`
  on exited threads, and completing a blocking `WaitAll` on pulse.
- **Wired Xbox 360 network reporting:** Matched all 52 steps of the console
  `XNet` startup reference (`XNetGetTitleXnAddr`, Ethernet link status,
  `XNetGetOpt` defaults, connect status, and suppressing spurious link-down
  notifications on new listeners).
- **Additional console reference suites:** Captured hardware references for
  TLS slots, `ex_rwlock`, event priority boost, float formatting, wait wake
  order, 2D texture fetches and mip-level selection.

### Metal renderer and scaling internals

- **Lossless RT compression and 2x/3x scaling pipeline:** Defaulted
  `XE_METAL_RT_COMPRESSIBLE` on for single-sample color targets, alongside
  scaled resolve forwarding, tight materialize passes, resolve-clear pack
  skipping, extended direct color/depth resolves and fused
  resolve-plus-scatter kernels.
- **Apple Silicon TBDR tile-safety fix:** Tight materialize passes now
  require full attachment height before using `MTLLoadActionDontCare`,
  preventing Apple GPU hardware tiles from overwriting live rows below
  misaligned partial-height passes.
- **Global Metal EDRAM and memexport fixes:** Applied `PA_SC_WINDOW_OFFSET`
  when computing scissor range starts for predicated tiling; kept written
  depth targets bound when a color target aliases only the stencil byte;
  retained pixel shaders on depth-only float24 conversion draws; drained
  large (>16 MB) completed vertex-shader memexports at frame boundaries;
  and routed scaled array/slice resolves through CPU writeback.
- **Regression testing:** Expanded `xenia-gpu-conformance` to **826 test
  cases** (23.8M+ assertions), covering scaled EDRAM layout, direct and
  fused resolves, and partial-height TBDR materialize safety.

### Global kernel, memory, networking and audio fixes

- **Per-title `cache:` partition:** Remounts `\CACHE` at
  `<cache folder>/<title ID>` at title load rather than sharing one host
  folder across every game, preventing cross-title cache collisions (while
  keeping `cache0:` and `cache1:` shared for large game installs).
- **16 KB Apple Silicon host pages:** Re-derives host page access when
  4 KB guest virtual pages are released and recommitted on a previously
  narrowed host page, fixing spurious boot-time access faults.
- **Guest sockets and overlapped I/O:** Kept privileged-port remapping
  (`< 1024`) transparent across `sendto`, `recvfrom`, `getsockname` and
  `accept` with explicit BSD `sockaddr_in` layout and byte order; fixed
  POSIX `select()` native `nfds` bounds and error propagation; translated
  guest `setsockopt()` option widths/endianness; implemented
  `NetDll_WSAEventSelect`; and cleared `XAM_OVERLAPPED::context` on
  completion.
- **Audio and upstream parity:** Integrated FFmpeg's `xmaframes` frame-trim
  handling in XMA decoding, plus 59 upstream Xenia Canary and Edge fixes
  (big-endian `KAPC`/`KTHREAD` fields, LDI LZX decompression exports,
  virtual memory protection reporting, PowerPC `OE` multiply forms, and
  XAM/kernel return codes).

## Known issues

- **Rise of the Tomb Raider:** a single false-color frame can still flash
  now and then on M4 and newer Macs.
- **Marvel vs. Capcom 2** can freeze while loading characters.
- **Madden NFL 06** stays on its loading splash.
- **Medal of Honor: Warfighter** can crash about 25 seconds into the first
  mission.
- **Need for Speed: The Run** sometimes stops during the opening race on M1
  Macs.
- **Forza Horizon 2:** audio can stutter when many sounds play at once.
- **Fight Night Round 4:** the boxers' skin can still show a foil-like
  rainbow sheen on some boots.
- **The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim** crashes occasionally (about 1 boot in 5).
- **Jurassic: The Hunted:** a vertical seam can show in the opening walk
  with MetalFX upscaling on.
- **Fable III** can stop on a black screen while loading the game after
  character select, most often on M5 Macs (also in 1.0.x). Quitting and
  starting the game again usually gets past it.
- **The Darkness** can stay on a white screen at the start of the game on M1
  and M4 Macs. It plays on M5 Macs.
- See the [compatibility list](../compatibility.md) for every tested game.
